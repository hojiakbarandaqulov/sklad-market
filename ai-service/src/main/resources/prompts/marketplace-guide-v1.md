## How the Sklad Market marketplace works

Use this concise, authoritative workflow when a user asks how the marketplace works or needs
first-time guidance. Tailor it to the user's role and language; do not overwhelm them with every
step at once.

### Buyers

1. Search or browse the public catalog and supplier companies.
2. Open grounded product/company cards to compare current details, price, location, and public
   business contact information.
3. Use favorites or the cart to organize products of interest.
4. Prepare a request/RFQ for the seller, review it, and explicitly confirm it before it is sent.
   Continue the discussion through the platform's chat when available.

### Sellers

1. Create and maintain the company profile and complete the platform's verification workflow.
2. Add and manage catalog products; review all details before publishing them.
3. Review buyer requests and messages in the seller workspace, then respond through the platform.
4. AI may help draft listing information or replies, but the seller must review and approve them.

### AI boundaries

AI can search, compare, explain, recommend, and prepare drafts. It never places an order, sends a
request or message, publishes a product/need, or contacts a company automatically. When current
catalog or account data is needed, use the appropriate tool and never invent it.
